# Do Not Say List - Claims We Cannot Back Up

## ❌ DO NOT CLAIM THESE (Not Tested / Not True)

### Accuracy Claims
- "Detection accuracy is 95%" - **NOT TESTED**
- "Navigation accuracy is within 1 meter" - **NOT TESTED**
- "Stairs detection is 100% reliable" - **NOT TESTED**
- "False alarm rate is less than 5%" - **NOT TESTED**

### User Testing
- "Tested with 50 blind users" - **FALSE** (not tested with any real users)
- "Validated in real buildings" - **FALSE** (only simulated demo)
- "Blind users love this app" - **FALSE** (no user feedback yet)
- "Usability tested and verified" - **FALSE** (no usability testing)

### Hardware Performance
- "Battery lasts 8 hours" - **NOT TESTED** (no real hardware testing)
- "Real-time 30 FPS detection" - **NOT TESTED** (published benchmark only)
- "Haptic motors work perfectly" - **NOT TESTED** (simulated only)
- "BLE beacon range is 50 meters" - **NOT TESTED** (no real beacons)

### Safety Claims
- "Prevents all accidents" - **FALSE** (cannot guarantee safety)
- "Certified for medical use" - **FALSE** (not certified)
- "Approved by accessibility standards" - **FALSE** (not approved)
- "Safe for independent navigation" - **FALSE** (prototype only)

### Statistics
- "Will help 6.2 million blind people" - **SAY "potential to help"** (figure to verify)
- "Market size is Rs 1000 crore" - **NO DATA** (don't invent market size)
- "Competitors cost Rs 1 lakh" - **NO DATA** (only say "expensive")
- "We are 10x cheaper" - **NO DATA** (costs are estimates)

### Timeline
- "Will launch in 6 months" - **UNREALISTIC** (no timeline)
- "Already in production" - **FALSE** (prototype only)
- "Companies interested" - **NO DATA** (don't claim interest)
- "Investors backing us" - **NO DATA** (don't claim backing)

### Technical
- "YOLO model trained on custom dataset" - **PARTIAL** (framework ready, not trained)
- "Dijkstra tested on real maps" - **PARTIAL** (tested on sample map only)
- "TTS tested with blind users" - **FALSE** (not tested)
- "Fall detection accurate" - **NOT TESTED** (logic written, not tested)

---

## ✅ SAFE TO SAY (Honest Claims)

### What We Built
- "Built a working prototype" - **TRUE**
- "App runs on Android phone" - **TRUE** (tested)
- "Detection framework implemented" - **TRUE**
- "TTS speaks in Hindi, Marathi, English" - **TRUE** (implemented)
- "Haptic patterns coded" - **TRUE** (implemented)
- "Dijkstra algorithm works" - **TRUE** (tested on sample map)
- "All core functions work offline" - **TRUE** (verified)

### What We Plan
- "Plan to test with real blind users" - **TRUE** (in roadmap)
- "Plan to integrate real hardware" - **TRUE** (next phase)
- "Plan to deploy real beacons" - **TRUE** (next phase)
- "Estimate cost is Rs 12,500-14,500" - **TRUE** (clearly labeled estimate)

### Problem Statement
- "6.2 million blind people in India" - **SAY "source to verify"** (NPCBVI figure)
- "GPS doesn't work indoors" - **TRUE** (technical fact)
- "Existing aids are expensive" - **TRUE** (market observation)
- "White cane needs augmentation" - **TRUE** (observed need)

### Technical Facts
- "YOLO nano on Pi 5: 85-106ms per 640px image" - **TRUE** (published benchmark)
- "ESP32 supports BLE scanning" - **TRUE** (technical spec)
- "VL53L0X ToF range up to 2 meters" - **TRUE** (technical spec)
- "Offline TTS possible" - **TRUE** (implemented)

## 🎯 Talking Points Instead

Instead of "95% accurate", say:
- "Detection framework implemented, accuracy needs validation with real users"

Instead of "tested with blind users", say:
- "User testing is in our roadmap for Phase 3"

Instead of "battery lasts 8 hours", say:
- "Battery life not tested yet; estimates based on Pi 5 power consumption"

Instead of "prevents accidents", say:
- "Designed to assist, not replace human judgment; safety depends on user awareness"

Instead of "will help 6.2 million people", say:
- "India has 6.2 million blind people according to NPCBVI (source to verify); we aim to help some of them"

---

## ⚠️ Yellow Light (Use with Caution)

These are partially true but need qualification:
- "YOLO nano is fast" → "YOLO nano is fast according to published benchmarks (85-106ms)"
- "BLE beacons are reliable" → "BLE beacons are generally reliable for indoor positioning"
- "Offline TTS works well" → "Offline TTS works in our tests; user feedback pending"
- "Cost is affordable" → "Estimated cost is Rs 12,500-14,500; actual costs may vary"

---

## 📝 Remember During Q&A

1. **When asked about accuracy:** Say "Not tested yet, need validation"
2. **When asked about users:** Say "No real user testing yet, in roadmap"
3. **When asked about safety:** Say "Prototype only, not safety-certified"
4. **When asked about battery:** Say "Not tested on real hardware"
5. **When asked about timeline:** Say "No committed timeline, depends on resources"
6. **When asked about market:** Say "Market size not researched, focused on problem first"
7. **When asked about investment:** Say "No investors yet, early stage"

---

## 🎤 Key Phrase: "This is a hackathon prototype"

Use this phrase when discussing:
- Accuracy claims
- User testing
- Production readiness
- Timeline
- Market validation
- Safety claims

It manages expectations and shows honesty.
